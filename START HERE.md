# CEZ Job Finder

## Do this now

1. Find the chat box on the right. (Using ChatGPT? Click its icon on the left first.)
2. Asked to log in? Log in, then come back here.
3. Type **set me up** and press Enter.

The AI asks a few questions, then shows your first jobs. You never type commands or edit files.

## Then just ask

- **Jobs:** "Any new jobs?" · "Why is job 3 on my list?" · "Stop showing jobs from Acme Staffing"
- **Resume:** "How good is my resume?" · "Help me add numbers to my resume" · "Change my phone number"
- **One job:** "Make my resume for job 3" · "Why did you change that?" · "Help me apply to job 3"
- **Anything:** "Who can see my information?" · "Why?"

## Where the jobs come from

**freehire.me** - a free public list of job postings, gathered from companies' own hiring pages
and job boards. Each job links to the real posting. New ones checked every morning.

## Your resume

- Your facts: **Resume details.yml** in My Resume. Change a line, or tell the chat.
- Nothing made up. Employers, titles, dates stay as you gave them.
- One resume per job, in **My Jobs**. You approve every reworded line first.
- Why it's written that way: **[What makes a good resume](Guides/What%20makes%20a%20good%20resume.md)**

## Who sees what

**Everything in the file list on the left stays on this computer.**

| What | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| Your resume, jobs, settings | Only you - this computer |
| What you're searching for (not your resume) | freehire.me, to find jobs |
| Your resume + jobs you ask about | Your own AI chat account |
| What you apply with | That employer, when you click Submit |

**AI training:** on a personal Claude or ChatGPT plan, your chats may help train future AI
unless you switch it off. How, and what it doesn't cover: **[Keep your chats out of AI training](Guides/Keep%20your%20chats%20out%20of%20AI%20training.md)**

**If CEZ Job Finder itself breaks:** the AI can repair the program and asks before sharing that
repair with its maker. Only the repaired program code is sent - never your resume, jobs,
settings or anything personal.

## Coming back

Double-click **CEZ Job Finder** on your Desktop, or click the morning notification.
