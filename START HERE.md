# CEZ Job Finder

## Do this now

1. Look at the chat on the right. Asked to sign in? Sign in with your AI account. (GitHub
   Copilot: then pick **Claude Sonnet** in the model list under the chat box.)
2. Click the empty box at the bottom of the chat.
3. Type **set me up** and press Enter.

The AI asks a few questions, then shows your first jobs. You never type commands or edit files.

No chat on the right? Close CEZ Job Finder and open it again from its icon on your Desktop.

## After that

Next time you open CEZ Job Finder, the **Today** page opens here instead: what's waiting on
you, the newest jobs, and buttons for what to say next.

- **[What you can ask](Guides/What%20you%20can%20ask.md)** - things to say, where the jobs come from, how your resume is made
- **[Who sees what](Guides/Who%20sees%20what.md)** - everything in the file list stays on this computer
- **[Terms](https://jobs.enrriquez.com/terms.html)** - free, provided as is; read everything the AI writes before you send it
