# Following up

A job you sent and heard nothing about shows under **Follow up** on Today.

## When

- **Applied:** about 3 weeks with no reply.
- **Heard back:** about 2 weeks after you last talked.
- **Interview:** about 12 days after it.

Common advice from recruiters plus a few small counts - not a rule. Want a different wait? Tell
the chat.

## What to do

- Say "write a follow-up for job 3". A short note appears in that job's folder.
- Send it yourself, from your own email, to the person you were in touch with. Nothing is sent for you.
- Then say "I followed up on job 3". The job leaves the list for a while.

One note per silence. Still no reply after that? Today asks if the job is closed.

## Silence is common

Many employers never write back. No reply says nothing about you.
