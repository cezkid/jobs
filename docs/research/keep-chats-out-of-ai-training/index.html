Keep your chats out of AI training: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot

By Cesar Enrriquez-Zuniga · Published 3 October 2026

A guide. Every step was checked against the company's own help page in October 2026.

Short answer

  • Personal plans of ChatGPT, Claude, GitHub Copilot and Gemini can train on your chats; one main switch each turns most of it off (Maker's docs) (OpenAI 2026a; Anthropic 2026c; GitHub 2026b; Google 2026b). See what the switch doesn't do, below.
  • Work and school plans don't train on chats by default (Maker's docs) (OpenAI 2026a; Anthropic 2026d; GitHub 2026b).
  • Switch stops future training; training already done stays (Maker's docs) (Anthropic 2026c; OpenAI 2026a).
  • Thumbs up or down can still send that whole chat to training (Maker's docs) (OpenAI 2026a; Anthropic 2026b).
  • AI models can repeat rare text from their training data (Lab studies, web text, not chats) (Carlini et al. 2021; Nasr et al. 2025).

Why does this matter for a job seeker?

A resume is personal data. It names you, your employers, your schools and your dates. Job seekers also type pay, work permits and gaps into AI chats.

On personal plans, the companies may use those chats to train future AI models. ChatGPT "improves by further training on the conversations people have with it," says OpenAI, unless you opt out (OpenAI 2026b). GitHub started using Copilot Free, Pro and Pro+ interactions, chats and code, for training from April 24, 2026, unless you opt out (GitHub 2026a). Anthropic asked Claude Free, Pro and Max users to choose in 2025 (Anthropic 2025). If you clicked through that pop-up, check where your switch is now.

Training data can come back out. In a 2021 test, researchers pulled hundreds of exact passages from an older AI model's training text (Carlini et al. 2021). Those passages included names, phone numbers and email addresses already public online (Carlini et al. 2021). A passage could leak even when it appeared in just one document (Carlini et al. 2021). A follow-up study pulled thousands of training passages from ChatGPT with a special trick (Nasr et al. 2025).

Both studies used text from public web pages, not users' chats. No study we found shows a chat from one user coming out in another user's answers. The companies say they reduce personal details before training. OpenAI says it takes "steps to reduce the amount of personal information" in training data (OpenAI 2026b). Anthropic says it filters or hides sensitive data (Anthropic 2025). It also separates feedback chats from your account before using them (Anthropic 2026b).

For you, the switch is a cheap step. In ChatGPT, your chats stay in your history with it off (OpenAI 2026a).

How do I stop ChatGPT training on my chats?

On ChatGPT Free, Go, Plus and Pro, the switch is called Improve the model for everyone (OpenAI 2026a).

  1. On the website, open your account menu and select Settings (OpenAI 2026a).
  2. Select Data controls (OpenAI 2026a).
  3. Select Improve the model for everyone, turn it off, and select Done (OpenAI 2026a).

On the phone app, open the sidebar, tap your profile icon, then Data controls (OpenAI 2026a). The choice follows your account across devices when you're signed in (OpenAI 2026a). The same switch covers Codex tasks on a personal plan; Codex's "Include environments" setting is separate (OpenAI 2026a).

OpenAI also offers a "do not train on my content" button in its privacy portal (OpenAI 2026b). Either route is enough; you don't need both (OpenAI 2026a).

Temporary Chat is a one-off option. Those chats don't appear in your history and aren't used for training (OpenAI 2026a). OpenAI may keep them for up to 30 days for safety (OpenAI 2026a).

For you, turn the switch off once, then use Temporary Chat for anything you don't want in your history or in training.

How do I stop Claude training on my chats?

On Claude Free, Pro and Max, the switch is called Help Improve our AI models (Anthropic 2026c).

  1. Select your name, then Settings (Anthropic 2026c).
  2. Select Privacy. The direct address is claude.ai/settings/data-privacy-controls (Anthropic 2026c).
  3. Turn Help Improve our AI models off (Anthropic 2026c).

The switch also covers Claude Code on those plans (Anthropic 2026c). Anthropic made this a choice on August 28, 2025, with a deadline of October 8, 2025 (Anthropic 2025).

With the switch on, a de-identified copy of your chats can stay up to 5 years in training data (Anthropic 2026a). For everyone, a deleted chat leaves Anthropic's main systems within 30 days (Anthropic 2026a). Chats flagged by safety checks are kept up to 2 years (Anthropic 2026a). Incognito chats are never used for training, whatever the switch says (Anthropic 2026b).

For you, the switch keeps your chats out of that 5-year training store.

How do I stop GitHub Copilot training on my chats?

This is GitHub Copilot, the coding tool. Microsoft Copilot has its own settings, not covered here.

On Copilot Free, Pro, Pro+ and Max, the setting is Allow GitHub to use my data for AI model training (GitHub 2026b).

  1. On github.com, click your profile picture, then Copilot settings (GitHub 2026b).
  2. Open the Allow GitHub to use my data for AI model training menu (GitHub 2026b).
  3. Click Disabled (GitHub 2026b).

The setting covers Copilot Chat and suggestions in your code editor, including your code and file names (GitHub 2026a). If you opted out of data collection before, GitHub kept that choice (GitHub 2026a).

Students on Copilot Student and teachers on free Copilot Pro are not affected (GitHub 2026c). Neither are members of a paid company account (GitHub 2026c). Business and Enterprise plans aren't used for training under GitHub's contracts, so the setting doesn't show there (GitHub 2026b).

GitHub may share this data with "companies in our corporate family including Microsoft" (GitHub 2026a). Other AI companies whose models run inside Copilot don't get it for their own training (GitHub 2026a; GitHub 2026c). Hired service firms may help GitHub with training, under contract (GitHub 2026c).

For you, a paid personal plan does not protect your chats. For most personal plans, only the switch does.

How do I stop Gemini training on my chats?

Gemini ties training to a setting called Keep Activity (Google 2026a).

  1. Go to gemini.google.com (Google 2026a).
  2. Click Settings & help, then Activity (Google 2026a).
  3. Click On, then Turn off or Turn off and delete activity (Google 2026a).

Keep Activity is on by default for users 18 and over (Google 2026a). With it off, Google keeps new chats with your account for up to 72 hours (Google 2026b). Those chats aren't used to train AI models unless you send feedback (Google 2026b).

With it off, only a few connected apps keep working (Google 2026a). Google still has people review some chats for safety, even with the setting off (Google 2026b). Reviewed chats are kept for up to 3 years, apart from your account (Google 2026b). Gemini also has a Temporary chat; those chats aren't used for training and are kept up to 72 hours (Google 2026b).

For you, Gemini's switch costs you your saved chats. ChatGPT keeps your history with its switch off (OpenAI 2026a).

What doesn't the switch do?

Past training stays. Anthropic says so outright; the others don't say. Anthropic says data "will still be included in model training that has already started" (Anthropic 2026c). Anthropic stops using older stored chats in future training runs (Anthropic 2026c). Claude chats from before the 2025 change were not used unless you reopened them (Anthropic 2025).

Your history stays. Turning off ChatGPT's switch doesn't delete or hide saved chats (OpenAI 2026a). Delete a chat separately if you want it gone (OpenAI 2026a).

Feedback can send the whole chat. On ChatGPT, a thumbs up or down means "the entire conversation" may be used to train models (OpenAI 2026a). That holds even after you opt out (OpenAI 2026a). Anthropic may train on feedback too; it separates feedback from your account and keeps it up to 5 years (Anthropic 2026b). In Gemini, feedback also sends your last 24 hours of chats (Google 2026b). People review it, and it is kept up to 3 years (Google 2026b).

Safety review goes on. Claude chats flagged by safety checks may still be used to improve those checks (Anthropic 2026c). OpenAI keeps even Temporary Chats up to 30 days for safety (OpenAI 2026a). Google has people review some Gemini chats even with its setting off (Google 2026b).

Gemini's switch has a gap. Google says its Gemini settings don't cover chats it turns into anonymized data to improve its services (Google 2026b).

Your name still reaches the AI. The switch controls training, not reading. The AI has to read your resume to help with it.

For you, skip the thumbs buttons on any chat that holds your resume.

What about work, school and developer accounts?

Business plans are the opposite: no training by default.

  • OpenAI doesn't train on ChatGPT Business, Enterprise or Edu workspaces by default (OpenAI 2026a).
  • Anthropic's 2025 change excluded Claude for Work, Claude for Education and its developer platform (Anthropic 2025). Those are used for training only if you send feedback or agree to it (Anthropic 2026d).
  • GitHub doesn't train on Copilot Business or Enterprise data (GitHub 2026b).
  • OpenAI's developer platform isn't used for training unless the customer opts in (OpenAI 2026b).
  • Work or school Gemini is set by your admin (Google 2026a).

Feedback is the exception here too. On Claude business plans, thumbs up or down can still send a chat to training (Anthropic 2026d).

For you, a work login trains less, but your employer sets its rules and may see your chats (OpenAI 2026a). Job-hunt on your own account.

What we don't know

  • Whether a resume typed into a chat could come back out in someone else's answer. The leak studies used public web text, not chats (Carlini et al. 2021; Nasr et al. 2025).
  • How well the companies remove personal details before training. Their pages say they try; no outside check was found (OpenAI 2026b).
  • Whether the switches work as described. We can't test that from outside; this guide relies on the companies' own pages.
  • How long the menus stay where they are. Menus move; we re-check these steps every three months.

What helps

  • Turn the training switch off on every AI app you use for job hunting. It takes a few clicks each.
  • Skip thumbs up, thumbs down and "send feedback" on chats with your resume (OpenAI 2026a; Anthropic 2026b).
  • Use a temporary or incognito chat for one-off questions with personal details (OpenAI 2026a; Anthropic 2026b).
  • Delete chats you don't need. On Claude, deleted chats leave Anthropic's main systems within 30 days, unless flagged for safety or held for legal reasons (Anthropic 2026a).
  • Leave out what the AI doesn't need. Your street address, birth date and ID numbers rarely help a resume; that is common advice, not a study finding.

How CEZ Job Finder uses this

  • CEZ Job Finder runs inside your own AI chat: Claude, ChatGPT or GitHub Copilot. Your resume is read there.
  • At setup, it asks once whether you want your chats kept out of training, and walks you through the switch.
  • It never asks you to rate a chat, because feedback can send that chat to training.
  • Only you can change the switch. No setting in CEZ Job Finder reaches your AI account.
