Research on AI and resumes

What studies say about AI, hiring and resumes. Plain words, short answers first.

Every claim carries a label for how strong it is. Every claim from a study links to that study.

Labels run from big study, small study and survey to vendor survey, lab study, law, convention and our own tests.

Written with AI help, checked by a fresh AI review and by Cesar. How the articles are found, written, checked and corrected: How we research.

Evidence labels

Big study
Many studies pooled into one answer, or researchers sent thousands of real applications to real employers
Small study
Real applications, a few hundred per group
Survey
People were asked, and the method is published
Vendor survey
Asked by a company with something to sell
Lab study
Raters or AI models judged resumes, not real employers
Law
The law's own text, with the date we checked it
Real records
Real hiring records studied after the fact; shows a link, not a cause
Maker's docs
How a product is built to work, from its maker, with the date we read it
Court record
A court filing or order; claims in a lawsuit are not proof
News report
A news story, used only for an event such as a case filed
Convention
Career guides agree, but no study tested it
Our measurement
We ran the test ourselves; the article says how
