Research on AI and resumes

What studies say about AI, hiring and resumes. Plain words, short answers first.

  • Age discrimination in hiring: when it starts, what helps

    US tests find little or no drop in employer replies at 40; it shows around 50. With age stated, Swedish employers reply less from the early 40s.

    Published October 4, 2026. Updated October 7, 2026.

  • AI hiring laws in 2026: what employers must tell you

    New York City and Illinois (AI video interviews) require AI notice; the EU, for fully automated decisions. Colorado, California and Connecticut follow.

    Published October 4, 2026. Updated October 7, 2026.

  • Do cover letters still matter now that AI writes them?

    Often yes – many forms we checked had a letter box, and a 2019–20 test found more callbacks with one. AI-written letters now tell employers less.

    Published October 4, 2026. Updated October 7, 2026.

  • Does an employment gap on your resume hurt?

    Short gaps showed no clear cost in the biggest review; long ones cut replies. A reason line helped in one test, not in another. The bias is the employer’s.

    Published October 4, 2026. Updated October 7, 2026.

  • Hiring discrimination: what studies show, what helps

    Field tests find employers reply less to some names, ages and histories. Bias varies by employer. No resume change has been shown to remove it.

    Published October 4, 2026. Updated October 7, 2026.

  • Are two-column resumes ATS friendly? A 7-layout PDF test

    We read one resume in 7 layouts with 3 free PDF readers, not an employer’s system. Our plain page never broke; spaced-out headings always did.

    Published October 4, 2026. Updated October 7, 2026.

  • Is AI resume screening biased? What the studies show

    AI models judge resumes differently by name, gender, age and disability in tests. The direction changes by model and test. Real-hiring data is scarce.

    Published October 3, 2026. Updated October 7, 2026.

  • Can employers tell if AI wrote your resume?

    Mostly no, in tests with older AI models: most people couldn’t spot AI writing. About half of hiring managers say they’d care.

    Published October 3, 2026. Updated October 7, 2026.

  • Do ATS reject 75% of resumes? Where the number came from

    No study shows ATS reject 75% of resumes. The figure traces to a 2012 sales claim. We counted the screening questions on 143 application forms.

    Published October 3, 2026. Updated October 7, 2026.

  • Opt out of AI training: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot and Gemini

    ChatGPT, Claude, GitHub Copilot and Gemini can train on personal-plan chats. One switch in each app’s settings turns most of it off from now on.

    Published October 3, 2026. Updated October 7, 2026.

  • What makes a good resume? The evidence, rule by rule

    Resume rules sorted by evidence. Long gaps cost callbacks; typos lower recruiter ratings. Most format rules are career-guide convention, not tested.

    Published October 3, 2026. Updated October 7, 2026.

Every claim carries a label for how strong it is. Every claim from a study links to that study.

Labels run from big study, small study and survey to vendor survey, lab study, law, convention and our own tests.

Written with AI help, checked by a fresh AI review and by Cesar. How the articles are found, written, checked and corrected: How we research.

Evidence labels

Big study
Many studies pooled into one answer, or researchers sent thousands of real applications to real employers
Small study
Real applications, a few hundred per group
Survey
People were asked, and the method is published
Vendor survey
Asked by a company with something to sell
Lab study
Raters or AI models judged resumes, not real employers
Law
The law’s own text, with the date we checked it
Real records
Real hiring records studied after the fact; shows a link, not a cause
Maker’s docs
How a product is built to work, from its maker, with the date we read it
Court record
A court filing or order; claims in a lawsuit are not proof
News report
A news story, used only for an event such as a case filed
Convention
Career guides agree, but no study tested it
Our measurement
We ran the test ourselves; the article says how
