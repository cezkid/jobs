Privacy

Updated 2 October 2026

Short version

  • No account. No tracking. Nothing sent to us.
  • Your resume, jobs and settings stay on your computer.

What leaves your computer

  • freehire.me - your search words, to find jobs; which job from your list you make a resume for or ask about, to get the whole posting or see if it's still open. Not your resume.
  • Your AI (Claude, ChatGPT or GitHub Copilot) - your resume and what you tell it, on your own account. Personal plans may train on chats unless you switch it off.
  • Employers - what you apply with, only when you click Save or Submit.
  • Your email provider - only if you turn on email alerts; sent to yourself.
  • The maintainer - only a program fix, only if you say yes. Never your files.

Other companies' rules apply

  • Your AI's VS Code add-on follows that company's privacy policy.
  • VS Code (Microsoft) sends usage data by default. Turn off: Settings, search "telemetry".
  • Installing and updating downloads from GitHub, Microsoft and Astral. They see your IP address.
  • This page is hosted by GitHub, which logs visits. We add no cookies, analytics or files from other sites.

Delete everything

Ask the chat to turn off the daily check, then delete the CEZ Job Finder folder. Chats live in your AI account - delete them there.

Questions

Ask on GitHub. The code is public, so anyone can check this.
