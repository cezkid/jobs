Privacy

Updated October 6, 2026

Short version

  • No account. No tracking. Nothing sent to us.
  • Your resume, jobs, settings and notes about you stay on your computer.

What leaves your computer

WhoWhat they getWhen
freehire.meYour search words; the company names on your job list; which job from your list you make a resume for or ask about. Not your resume.To find jobs, get the whole posting, or see if it’s still open. Each morning, to find each listed company’s website.
Your AI (Claude, ChatGPT or GitHub Copilot)Your resume, the postings you work on and what you tell it, on your own account – a note you saved about yourself only when it’s used. Claude also gets your Today page’s job titles and companies as a chat opens, and the words and screenshots of pages it reads in your Chrome to fill a Workday application. GitHub Copilot gets the words and screenshots of pages in the CEZ Job Finder window that it opens or you share with it.While you chat. Personal plans may train on chats unless you switch it off.
EmployersWhat you apply with.When you click Save or Submit. Many sites get your resume as soon as it’s chosen; some get other parts as they’re filled (Ashby: each answer as its box is filled, a town or school letter by letter as it’s typed), or your email (some also name and phone) at their first Continue or Next. The AI tells you first.
Your email providerThe morning email, sent to yourself.Only if you turn on email alerts.
The maintainerA fix to the program’s code. Never your files.Only if you say yes.

Nothing else from the app leaves your computer.

Other companies’ rules apply

  • Your AI’s VS Code add-on follows that company’s privacy policy.
  • VS Code (Microsoft) sends usage data by default. If the installer put VS Code on your computer, the app turns that off. On a VS Code you already had: Settings, search “telemetry”.
  • Employers’ hiring sites run their own analytics. JazzHR sends your name, email, phone and town to its analytics company, Gainsight, when you click Submit – for about 1 in 100 applicants.
  • Installing and updating downloads from GitHub, Microsoft and Astral. They see your IP address.
  • This site is hosted by GitHub, which logs visits. We add no cookies, analytics or files from other sites.

Delete everything

Ask the chat to turn off the daily check and clear the sign-ins from pages you opened in the app’s window – VS Code keeps them outside the folder. Then delete the CEZ Job Finder folder.

Folder already gone? Paste %APPDATA%\Code\User\workspaceStorage into File Explorer’s address bar (Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/Code/User/workspaceStorage into Finder’s Go to Folder, Shift-Cmd-G). Delete the folder in it whose workspace.json names your CEZ Job Finder folder.

Chats live in your AI account – delete them there.

Claude also keeps a copy of each chat on your computer, outside the folder, for its chat history. It deletes each one after 30 days.
