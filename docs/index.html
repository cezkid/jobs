What it does

New jobs every morning

From freehire.me, a free public list gathered from companies' own hiring pages and job boards. Any field. Each job says why it's on your list.

A resume for any job you pick

One page, set to fit. Nothing made up: employers, titles and dates stay as you gave them. You approve every changed line.

Applications filled, you press Submit

Greenhouse, Ashby and UKG forms, and Workday with Claude. Others get answers ready to paste.

Help after you apply

Interview practice, follow-up emails, and a Today page with what's waiting on you.

What stays private

  • No account. Nothing uploaded to us.
  • Your resume, jobs and settings stay on your computer.
  • The job search gets your search words and which job from your list you work on - never your resume.
  • Your own AI plan reads your resume to help.
  • Setup shows how to keep your chats out of AI training.

Questions

Is this a website?

No. It's an app you install on your Windows or Mac computer. Your resume and jobs stay there.

Is it free?

The app is free. You need one AI plan: Claude Pro or Max, ChatGPT Plus or Pro, or GitHub Copilot Pro ($10 a month). Copilot's free plan can't make tailored resumes.

Use Nova, Poe or another all-in-one AI app?

Those apps can't run programs on your computer, so they can't run CEZ Job Finder. GitHub Copilot Pro ($10 a month) works like them - several AIs in one plan - and runs here. Pick Claude Sonnet as its AI.

Does it apply for me?

No. It fills the form; you check it and press Submit.

Will it make things up on my resume?

No. Employers, titles and dates stay as you gave them. You approve every reworded line.

What do I need?

Windows 10 or 11, or a Mac. About 5 minutes. No administrator password.

How do I install on a Mac?

Open Terminal (Spotlight, type Terminal). Paste curl -fsSL https://jobs.enrriquez.com/mac | bash and press Enter.

Is it safe to paste a line?

The line downloads this site's install script and runs it for you only. Read it first: Windows, Mac. All the code is public on GitHub.

How do I remove it?

Ask the chat to turn off the daily check, then delete the CEZ Job Finder folder.
