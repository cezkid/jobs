Your resume, made for one job

Pick a job. The chat rewrites your resume for it, in the posting’s own words where your experience backs them.

  • Nothing made up. A skill you don’t have is told to you, not added.
  • You approve every changed line.
  • One or two full pages, set to fit.

Who sees what

No account. Nothing sent to us. Your resume, jobs and settings stay on your computer.

Who What they get When

  1. freehire.me

    What they get: Your search words, which job from your list you make a resume for or ask about, and the company names on it. Never your resume.

    When: To find jobs and each company’s website, get the whole posting, or see if it’s still open.

  2. Your own AI account

    What they get: Your resume, the postings you work on and what you tell it, in Claude, ChatGPT or GitHub Copilot.

    When: While you chat. Personal plans may train on chats unless you switch it off.

  3. An employer

    What they get: What you apply with.

    When: When you click Save or Submit. Some sites get a file, an answer or your contact details sooner. The AI tells you first.

  4. Your email provider

    What they get: The morning email, sent to yourself.

    When: Only if you turn on email alerts.

  5. The maintainer

    What they get: A fix to the program’s code. Never your files.

    When: Only if you say yes.

Nothing else from the app leaves your computer. VS Code, your AI’s add-on and the installer follow their own companies’ rules: Privacy, in full.

    Resume rules sorted by evidence. Long gaps cost callbacks; typos lower recruiter ratings. Most format rules are career-guide convention, not tested.

  • Do ATS reject 75% of resumes? Where the number came from

    No study shows ATS reject 75% of resumes. The figure traces to a 2012 sales claim.

  • Is AI resume screening biased? What the studies show

    AI models judge resumes differently by name, gender, age and disability in tests. The direction changes by model and test.

Questions

Is this a website?

No. CEZ Job Finder is an app you install on your Windows or Mac computer. It looks for jobs in any field, remote or near you, on the freehire.me job search; setup counts the US jobs that match. Your resume and jobs stay on your computer.

Is it free?

The app is free. You need one AI plan: Claude Pro or Max, ChatGPT Plus or Pro, or GitHub Copilot Pro ($10 a month). Copilot’s free plan can’t make tailored resumes.

Can I use Nova, Poe or another all-in-one AI app?

Those apps can’t run programs on your computer, so they can’t run CEZ Job Finder. GitHub Copilot Pro ($10 a month) works like them – several AIs in one plan – and runs here. Pick Claude Sonnet as its AI.

Does it apply for me?

No. It fills the form; you check it and press Submit.

Will it make things up on my resume?

No. Employers, titles and dates stay as you gave them. You approve every reworded line.

What do I need?

Windows 10 or 11, or a Mac. About 5 minutes. No administrator password.

How do I install on a Mac?

Open Terminal (Spotlight, type Terminal). Paste curl -fsSL https://jobs.enrriquez.com/mac | bash and press Return.

Is it safe to paste a line?

The line downloads this site’s install script and runs it for you only. Read it first: Windows, Mac. All the code is public on GitHub.

How do I remove it?

Ask the chat to turn off the daily check and clear the sign-ins from pages opened in the app’s window – VS Code keeps them outside the folder. Then delete the CEZ Job Finder folder. More
