Job Finder

Finds new jobs that match what you want, tells you each morning when new ones arrive, and makes a version of your resume for any job you pick. You just talk to it in plain English.

Open this link on your Windows or Mac computer to install Job Finder.

You need: a Windows or Mac computer, and a paid Claude (Pro or Max) or ChatGPT (Plus or Pro) account.

1. Open PowerShellTerminal

Click the Start button, type PowerShell, then click Windows PowerShell in the results. A window opens with a blinking cursor.

Click the magnifying glass at the top-right of your screen, type Terminal, then click Terminal in the results. A window opens with a blinking cursor.

2. Copy this line into that window

Click Copy, then click inside the window and right-click to pastechoose Edit > Paste. Press Enter.

On a Mac? On Windows?

3. Answer one question

It asks which AI you pay for. Type 1 for Claude or 2 for ChatGPT, then press Enter.

4. Wait, then sign in

The window shows progress (about 5 minutes). Don't close it. VS Code opens by itself. Click Sign in on the right-hand panel, then press Enter.

Something went wrong?

Do it again from step 1 - it's safe to repeat. Your resume and settings are kept.

What stays private? Your resume, job folders and search settings stay on your computer. Job searches send only your search settings to freehire.me. Your resume is read by your own Claude or ChatGPT account while it helps you. Job Finder is free and open source: github.com/cezkid/jobs.
