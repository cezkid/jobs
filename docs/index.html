CEZ Job Finder

A free job-search helper on your computer. Needs a paid Claude or ChatGPT plan.

  • Finds new jobs that fit you, every day
  • Tailors your resume to any job
  • Fills the application - you press Submit
Private: no account, nothing uploaded to us.
  • Resume and settings stay on your computer
  • Job searches send only your search words
  • Your own AI plan reads your resume to help
  • Setup shows you how to switch off AI training on your chats

Open this page on your Windows or Mac computer to install.

  1. Open Windows PowerShell

    Open Terminal

    Start menu, type PowerShell

    Spotlight, type Terminal

  2. Paste this and press Enter

  3. Type 1 for Claude or 2 for ChatGPT

    VS Code opens in about 5 minutes. Click Sign in.

· Stuck? Run it again - your files are kept.
