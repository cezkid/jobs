Cesar Enrriquez-Zuniga makes CEZ Job Finder, a free app that finds jobs and makes a resume for each one.

Not a recruiter or lawyer: the Research section summarizes published studies and says how strong each is.

## How the articles are made

AI tools help find sources and write drafts. A fresh AI session checks every claim, and Cesar reads and approves each article before it goes up. The full process is on [How we research](methods.md).
